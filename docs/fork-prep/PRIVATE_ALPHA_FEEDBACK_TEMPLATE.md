# Private Alpha Feedback Template

## Purpose

Use this template when the fork reaches a usable private alpha and you want consistent feedback from early users.

## Context To Share With Testers

This is an early version of a job discovery and prioritization product.

We want feedback on:

- whether it helps you find better jobs
- whether the filters make sense
- whether the prioritization feels useful
- where the product feels confusing or untrustworthy

## Tester Questions

### 1. First Impression

- What was your first impression of the product?
- What did you think it was supposed to do?

### 2. Discovery Quality

- Did you see jobs you would not normally find or notice?
- Were the jobs generally relevant to your search?
- Did the source coverage feel strong enough?

### 3. Filtering Quality

- Were the filters useful?
- Did anything feel missing compared with your normal job search flow?
- Did the product hide anything you wish it had shown?

### 4. Prioritization

- Did the ranking/order feel sensible?
- Were the reasons for why jobs were shown or prioritized clear enough?
- Did you trust the recommendations?

### 5. Workflow

- Did the product save you time?
- Was anything frustrating or tedious?
- What was confusing or unclear?

### 6. Trust

- Did anything feel surprising or opaque?
- Were there moments where you were unsure why a job appeared or disappeared?
- What would increase your trust in the product?

### 7. Overall Value

- Would you keep using this?
- What is the main reason you would or would not?
- If this were polished, would you consider paying for it?

## Internal Summary Fields

For each alpha user, capture:

- user type
- target role
- sources they rely on today
- biggest success
- biggest confusion
- biggest missing feature
- trust concerns
- willingness to keep testing

## Decision Rule

If users do not clearly say some version of "this helps me find and prioritize better jobs than my current workflow," do not assume the alpha is working just because scraping functions.
